How a Rekha story works

Three rules, every episode

01

A story you know

Panchatantra, Jataka, Aesop, Tenali Raman. The plot is familiar, so all your attention goes to the line.

02

One line, never lifted

Every frame is drawn by a single stroke. No cuts. When the story looks back, the line rewinds.

03

An ending for today

The last beat turns the old moral toward phones, cities, climate and money. The line takes on one colour, once.
